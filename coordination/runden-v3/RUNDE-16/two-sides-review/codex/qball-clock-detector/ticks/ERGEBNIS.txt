PHASE AND SIMPLE EXPLANATION
Nearest central-density tick offsets, fine grid, in units of referenceperiod:
weak .243865 mean, .0001865 std; strong .02452 mean,.17253 std;
detuned .000998 mean,.005256 std. Nearest-tick pairing wraps modulo a period:
these are NOT unwrapped phase-locking or absence-of-slip proofs.

For an externally prescribed F=A cos(rho t) and OmegaD=rho, the detector
ODE is X''+rho^2 X=alpha rho^2 A cos(rho t). With X(0)=alpha A,P(0)=0,
X=alpha A cos(rho t)+(alpha A rho t/2)sin(rho t).
Direct differentiation verifies it. Its resonant growing part has a quarter
period lag relative to F. Thus the measured approximately .244-cycle offset
is compatible with an ordinary driven oscillator, not evidence for a new
fundamental delay or time dimension. Central density is not exactly F,
and the actual coupled system includes backreaction; this formula is only
an interpretation under the explicitly suppressed-backreaction assumption.
It cannot predict unlimited growth or the strong-coupling trajectory.
