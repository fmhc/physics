Q-Ball density clock with reciprocal finite detector — 2026-10-02
Exploratory radial 3D (spherical l=0) continuum-model simulation, ten runs.
No Cartesian geometry, apparatus calibration or quantum measurement model.

OUTCOME
Weak resonant readout survives the predefined finite clock gate. Strong
resonant readout fails it, while a 30% detuned strong detector passes.
All three coupled arms resolve detector motion and an energy transfer relative
to the numerical energy-balance error on BOTH grids. This is an explicitly
coupled model with a prepared known mode, not a new self-generated clock.
The clock gate tests CENTRAL FIELD DENSITY. The X readout gate tests amplitude,
not tick regularity of the detector itself. A reliable detector-clock output
has therefore not yet been demonstrated.

LIMITS AND NEXT DISCRIMINATING STEP
The detector is one spherical collective degree of freedom, coupled instantly
to weighted density with prescribed gaussian footprint. It is not yet a local
relativistic detector field. Next useful step: replace it by a dynamical local
neutral field with explicit propagation, reciprocal source and open/closed
radiation threshold, and test whether readable signal and finite clock survive.
That is a NEW model/test, not performed here. Lossless detector exchange does
not give a persistent irreversible recording, nor a perpetuum mobile.
No change to the published/paper BIC or stability claims.
