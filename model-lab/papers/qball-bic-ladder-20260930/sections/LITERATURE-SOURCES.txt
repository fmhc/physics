Editorial boundaries
No Schott/Bohm historical claims, no first-BIC/first-Q-ball-excitation
claims, and no asserted infinite ladder. A quantitative novelty search
would require matching potential normalization, dimension, background
branch, angular sector and definition of spectral frequency. This bounded
source audit does not establish absence of prior realizations. It also
does not turn linear BIC existence into nonlinear persistence.
