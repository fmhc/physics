D1. Frequency-dependent mediator range
First identify the actual medium operator and source channel. In an
explicitly new linear massive three-dimensional mediator model below its
threshold, the decay constant would be
  kappa = sqrt(mu² - Omega²) / c_m,
rather than its static value mu/c_m. A static Yukawa fit is therefore not
generally sufficient for dynamic sources. This does not yet explain K3,
particularly its one-dimensional geometry or its transients. The formula
is a conditional illustration in a specified new model, not a fit or a
result for the existing experiment.

D2. Hamiltonian phase binding with memory
The proposed Hamiltonian is
  H = U n²/2 - K cos(phi) + (P² + Omega_m² Q²)/2 + lambda Q sin(phi).
For a positive oscillator frequency Omega_m, static elimination of Q gives
a small-phase stiffness K - lambda²/Omega_m². Positive stiffness and U>0
allow small bound phase oscillations. These do not constitute dissipative
synchronization or spatial self-binding. Dynamical elimination retains the
medium's initial data and a retarded kernel.
Editorial clarification: this is a separate phase/mediator toy model; its
canonical pair (phi,n) and its mediator pair (Q,P) are unrelated to the
projectors P,Q in the linear propositions in extension.tex.

E. Boundary of the Hawking analogy
The coordinator describes Hawking radiation as a quantum emission process
on a black-hole background, not a name for stored internal energy. With net
emission, the black hole loses mass. Source cited by the coordinator:
  Hawking (1975), https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02345020
Declared consultation: publisher abstract only; no new full-text check claimed.

The project's classical kinetic, gradient, and potential energies remain
field energy. A BIC suppresses selected radiation channels; it does not
establish a horizon, a Hawking temperature, or particle creation from a
quantum vacuum.

A possible later connection is mixing of positive- and negative-norm modes.
Quantization, in/out states, and Bogoliubov coefficients must be defined.
Two sidebands alone are not evidence. Classical stimulated wave conversion
must be separated from spontaneous quantum emission. A Hawking analogue
also requires the relevant horizon/redshift mechanism and its spectral
prediction; more general parametric creation can occur without a horizon.

References suggested by the coordinator, abstracts only:
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.04481 — norm/frequency conventions.
- https://arxiv.org/abs/1510.00621 — reported BEC analogue experiment.
They are not evidence of such emission in the project model. No publication
metadata beyond the supplied identifiers have been newly validated here.
