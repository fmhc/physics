Separate subject
The single-complex-field sextic Q-ball, U(S)=S-S²+S³/2, belongs to a separate
result strand. Do not identify it with the SU(3) triplet experiment,
Higgsportal-Q250, or a model with two independent complex fields.
The SU(3) manuscript retains its current focus. A radial BIC and angular
degeneracy do not establish a local SU(3) gauge theory.

Limited novelty claim
The coordinator's audit identifies a possible contribution in the specific
3D embedded-mode candidate and quantitative nonlinear radiation analysis.
Open/closed scattering channels, Feshbach resonances, BIC interference,
3+1D radial scattering equations, and nonlinear harmonic radiation already
have literature precedents. A limited search finding no identical precursor
does not establish global priority. Avoid claims of a first discovery or
new fundamental interaction.

Updated proof status: distinguish the records
The initial portion of NEUHEITSMATRIX describes the project's R44 strand:
a numerical degree, an interval nonzero result for I2 in the frozen R44
surrogate and declared input box, and certificate_pass=FALSE. This is not
a certified continuum result for the original profile and infinite tail.

The subsequent update explicitly says this is not the whole project status.
It reports a separate submitted continuum existence proof, BEWEIS-1,
for n=1, l=0, with completed internal Anthropic code reviews. Independent
verification and a second implementation remain pending in the supplied
record. This translation session has not inspected or validated that proof.
Do not suppress the reported author proof, call it independently confirmed,
or infer that it automatically transfers the R44 nonzero radiation result.

The companion PAPER-LEITER-EINARBEITUNG proposes a separate paper on fifteen
candidate locations with heterogeneous evidence. Width minima, interpolated
locations, winding checks, and the n=1 author proof must retain distinct
labels. Do not call all fifteen rigorous BICs. The n=11–15 checks are reported
as sequential predictions of width minima, without a winding test in
RUNDE-09. They are not a single unchanged five-point blind test or two
independent replications. Full data provenance is required before a new
manuscript can claim or tabulate these results.

Nonlinear scaling terminology
The supplied ladder note describes leading radiated power consistent with
fourth-order perturbation-amplitude scaling. Do not collapse radiation
amplitude and radiated power into an unspecified 'quadratic radiation' claim.
This is a terminology constraint drawn from the supplied records, not a new
calculation or confirmation of their scaling results.

Simulator inventory: reusable components, missing connections
According to WEBGPU-UMBAU, rotation-lab contains WGSL compute infrastructure
for small ODE systems, not the required 3D field stencil. field3d supplies a
CUDA double-precision Cartesian PDE framework for a different multi-field
model; its equations and periodic-neighbour handling require explicit
adaptation. Its saved-frame viewer is not a live stream. qball-explorer
provides the relevant single-field radial live reference, with spherical
symmetry imposed by the ansatz.

The missing pieces are the correct single-field 3D adapter, live data
connection, quantitative volume/isosurface display, and validation. These
are inventory findings, not completed functionality or measured performance.
Display thresholds are not physical shells, and rendering does not certify
the field dynamics or a BIC. The supplied plan distinguishes exploratory
browser precision from the remote high-precision reference. No application
code has been changed or run by this editorial session.

Disposition
Preserve the three German source snapshots alongside this summary.
No change to the frozen SU(3) v0.5 translation, no new second-paper draft,
no simulator implementation, and no physics or QA runs follow from this
status intake. The separately reported n=1 proof remains an attributed
