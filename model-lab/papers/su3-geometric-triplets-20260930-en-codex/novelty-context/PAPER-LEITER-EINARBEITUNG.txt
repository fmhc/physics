ARBEITSTITEL
A ladder of radiative cancellations in the breathing spectrum of
three-dimensional Q-balls.
Nach abgeschlossenem unabhängigen Beweisaudit kann der präzisere BIC-Titel
mit computer-assisted existence for the first embedded mode verwendet werden.
Keine pauschale Gleichsetzung aller 15 Breitenminima mit bewiesenen BICs.

HAUPTAUSSAGE / DIREKT VERWENDBARER ENGLISCHER ABSATZ
We investigate a sequence of radiative cancellations in the linearized
breathing spectrum of three-dimensional sextic Q-balls. At beta=1/2,
our numerical continuation identifies fifteen candidate locations, with
heterogeneous evidence ranging from resolved width minima to winding-number
checks. Sequential predictions successfully locate additional width minima
at indices 11–15. A thin-wall phase-matching description accounts for the
approximately uniform spacing of the inverse detuning from the thin-wall
threshold. A separate computer-assisted argument establishes existence of
the first radial embedded mode according to the proof package and its
completed internal code reviews; independent verification remains pending.
Finite-amplitude evolution and an independently solved forced radiation
problem show that linear trapping does not generally persist nonlinearly:
the leading resolved harmonic carries power consistent with fourth-order
amplitude scaling. These results distinguish a structured linear spectrum
from nonlinear stability and from a universal or infinite-ladder theorem.
