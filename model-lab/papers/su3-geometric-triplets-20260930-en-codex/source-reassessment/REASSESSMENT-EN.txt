ASSESSMENT
The four existing sources mainly provide context, not the scientific chain
of evidence for the proposed connection geometry -> field triplet ->
nonlinear SU(3) interaction -> QCD. The qualified citations in v0.5 are
defensible within limits, but their selection emphasizes the wrong related
areas. None of these four sources derives our entire mechanism. The paper
must distinguish its own construction, methodological links to the literature,
and established physics in nature more visibly.

EXISTING [1] Hammer, quant-ph/0402065v5
https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0402065
Current consultation: primary abstract/bibliography; an earlier detailed
audit of Sections II–IV and VI is available.
Supports atomic ring modes and subradiance. Different degrees of freedom,
open decay dynamics, and cyclic symmetry rather than the proposed SU(3)
symmetry. Decision: remove from the central argument; at most retain a brief
aside explicitly identified as an analogy. Not evidence for our conservative
graph model, our Q-ball, or stable composite particles.

EXISTING [2] PDG QCD, 2025, Section 9.1
https://pdg.lbl.gov/2025/reviews/rpp2025-rev-qcd.pdf
Primary review reopened; an earlier detailed audit is available.
Supports the definition and delineation of actual QCD. Decision: retain,
explicitly as a benchmark against which to distinguish our model. Global
U(3)/SU(3) of a finite mode sector is not local gauge dynamics. Our derivation
of trivial holonomy for links formed purely from complete bases remains our
own algebra under stated assumptions, not a PDG claim.

EXISTING [3] Kaminaga, 2602.09376v1
https://arxiv.org/html/2602.09376v1
Reopened: model/introduction; Sections 6–7 have already been examined twice,
separately.
Useful comparison for central-potential partial waves, but delta shells
rather than smooth Klein–Gordon wells. The tunneling theorem for the matched
l=0 limiting case does not establish l=1 transfer values. Decision: optional
comparison, not a central supporting reference. In our text, m-degeneracy
follows directly from separation of variables. The documented observations
about the proof do not establish that the entire preprint is wrong; they
prevent its use as an unchecked basis for certification. Do not look for a
replacement paper solely because its shells look similar.

EXISTING [4] Panin/Smolyakov, 1810.03558
https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.03558
Original metadata reopened; an earlier audit of full-text excerpts is available.
A two-field Wick–Cutkosky model, not our sextic single-field model.
Translation/VK considerations are methodologically useful; spectral results
cannot be transferred. Decision: supplement with more directly relevant
single-field work, or replace in the main text, for our specific treatment
of Q-ball perturbations. Do not use as evidence for the desired pair of
positive l=1 levels.

MORE DIRECT METHODOLOGICAL REFERENCES
Smolyakov, 1711.05730v2. Primary PDF: abstract, discussion of perturbations and
balance laws, and the stability assumption around Eqs. 44–47/Remark 4 were
read selectively.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1711.05730
Shows why the charge and energy of a Q-ball perturbation at second order
cannot consistently be obtained from the linear solution alone. Directly
supports our caveat concerning lifetime and background backreaction; does
not calculate our BIC or its radiation rate.

Smolyakov, 1906.02117. Primary PDF: introduction and Eqs. 1–8 read.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1906.02117
A general complex single-field potential and nonlinear corrections,
including nonoscillatory modes. Structurally much closer than Wick–Cutkosky.
Same author as the previous entry: complementary material, not independent
confirmatory evidence.

Kovtun/Nugaev/Shkerin, 1805.03518. Primary PDF: potential definitions and
Eqs. 14–16 read.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1805.03518
The coupled positive/negative-frequency channels and angular decomposition
are methodologically relevant. The potentials and regimes actually studied
are not automatically ours. Do not adopt the printed blanket origin
condition: for l>0, regular Frobenius asymptotics are decisive; a radial l=1
amplitude proportional to r does not generally have zero derivative at r=0.
Use: literature context for the coupled spectral problem, not BIC certification.

A BETTER STRUCTURAL ANALOGY FOR THREE COMPONENTS
Ho, cond-mat/9803231. Primary PDF: Eqs. 4–6 and ground-state discussion read.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/cond-mat/9803231
A spin-1 condensate with independent density and spin couplings. For a
Cartesian complex vector, our own identity is
  |F|² = S² - |a·a|²,    F = -i a* × a.
Our additional invariant thus belongs to the established spinor structure.
Three components do not automatically imply SU(3); the couplings decide.
Internal atomic spin, however, is not the same physical quantity as our
spatial l=1 mode index.

Peletminskii/Peletminskii/Slyusarenko, 1911.11391. Primary PDF: Eqs. 10–14 read.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1911.11391
In that construction, spin and quadrupole couplings have SU(3) symmetry
only when J=K; a Zeeman term can break it. Decision: a direct comparison for
the required matching of interactions. Not a reference for emergent QCD,
and not evidence that our geometry forces the couplings to be equal. The
publication still requires a separate bibliographic check against primary
metadata before it is included as a finalized bibliography entry.

IMPLICATIONS FOR OUR CONCEPT (THE COORDINATOR'S ASSESSMENT)
1. Equal linear exchange in three channels within the external spherical-shell
   potential remains a comprehensible model construction. Our own numerical
   calculations and algebra support it.
2. The origin and self-stabilization of the shells remain open. None of the
   cited papers closes this gap on our behalf.
3. Generic local nonlinearity distinguishes polarizations; this fits a spinor
   analogy better than a QCD analogy. Special tuning of the leading quartic
   term does not yet protect all higher orders or multiple radial branches.
4. The original Q-ball spectrum, including the exterior region and nonlinear
   radiation, remains a separate investigation. Our null finding in a finite
   box does not prove global absence.
5. Do not claim novelty for the triplet/exchange algebra. A possible project
   contribution is the concrete mapping between models, testable
   counterexamples, and controlled numerical calculations.
