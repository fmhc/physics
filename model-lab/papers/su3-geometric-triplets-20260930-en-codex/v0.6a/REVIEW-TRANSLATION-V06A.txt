The parent independently flagged the same issue. Recommended and now
observed correction: 'cube graphs', matching TeX. This avoids the graph-
theoretic ambiguity of 'cubic'; the specific cube graph is intended.

with HTML 'particle number'. The existing denial of a particle-count
interpretation is already understandable; this is not a missing caveat.

Lagrangian an action (Wirkung), while its notation paragraph correctly
calls it a Lagrangian. Both translations preserve this source-level

German manuscript already used one consistent term. Equation (11) itself
is translated without adding a time integral.
