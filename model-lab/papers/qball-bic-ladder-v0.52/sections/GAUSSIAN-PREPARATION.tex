\paragraph{Preparing a matched-energy and matched-charge width comparison.}
A restricted, analytically tractable preparation uses
$\phi=A\exp[-r^2/(2R^2)]$ and $\Pi=\ii Q\phi/(2I)$, where
$I=\int |\phi|^2\dd^d x=A^2\pi^{d/2}R^d$ and $Q\ne0$.
For the same potential in $d$ spatial dimensions its energy is
\[
 E_d(I,R,Q)=\frac{Q^2}{4I}
 +\left(1+\frac{d}{2R^2}\right)I
 -\frac{I^2}{(2\pi)^{d/2}R^d}
 +\frac{I^3}{2\,3^{d/2}\pi^d R^{2d}}.
\]
The chosen momentum minimizes kinetic energy at fixed field and charge;
it does not make the Gaussian a stationary solution.
At fixed $R,Q$ this expression has one positive-norm minimum
$E_{\min}(R,Q)$. Two prescribed widths $R_1,R_2$ can therefore share
$(E_0,Q)$ within this preparation family precisely when
$E_0\ge\max_j E_{\min}(R_j,Q)$.
Above each minimum there are two positive solutions for $I$; a comparison
must fix its branch in advance, for example the smaller-$I$ solution.
Actual discrete energies and charges must be checked separately.
The factors of $d$ describe spatial dimension, not additional field
components. This width comparison does not replace the disordered-spectrum
test above, and for $E_0<|Q|$ both admissible preparations already obey
the non-dilution bound. No formation run using this prescription is
reported here.
