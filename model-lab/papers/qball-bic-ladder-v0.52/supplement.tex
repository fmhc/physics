This Supplement records the nonlinear coefficient's dependency audit,
followed by exploratory driving, formation and numerical-control
material retained from the earlier manuscript. The complete linear
proof lemmas are in Appendix A of the main paper.
The latter material is not evidence for additional localized linear modes.
Equation and section references without an S prefix refer to the main paper.
The companion archive is distributed with release \texttt{qball-bic-v0.51}
(see the data availability statement of the main paper); text, data and
certificates are licensed under CC BY 4.0, code under the MIT License.
Historical records are preserved. The 0.51 preprint
repeated the three primary linear certificate runs with unchanged proof
code; it did not repeat the exploratory physical evolutions below.
Preprint 0.52 adds explanatory material only.

\section{Archive structure and evidential scope}\label{app:provenance}
The companion archive is distributed with an inventory mapping
original records to portable paths and SHA256 bindings distinguishing
historical records from derived export copies. The three primary linear
certificate runs were reproduced for preprint 0.51: their receipts agree
with the originals after omitting four timing fields, and all six point
and box step records are byte-identical. This does not validate a new
implementation or reproduce the planar-wall comparison and nonlinear
coefficient bound, which retain separate provenance. Failed sufficient
checks and adaptive repetitions remain recorded with their original status.

{\sloppy
The overview figure of the main paper (Fig.~\ref{fig:overview}) is
rendered from archived data and evaluations of formulas stated in the
paper, with no new simulation, fit or certificate run. Its data inputs
are the archive files \texttt{figures/modes/PROFILE.json} (SHA-256
prefix \texttt{9509436ad3fd5771}), \texttt{ladder/figure/figure-data.json}
(\texttt{1dcf9139e23bc316}) and \texttt{ladder/extension/sprossen.json}
(\texttt{4267791b7d2dfaae}), together with the midpoints of
Table~\ref{tab:proof-cases}. Panel~(a) also evaluates the thin-wall
formula, Eq.~\eqref{eq:thinwall}, and panel~(b) the function $V(f)$
of the figure caption; panel~(c) uses only the certified midpoints.
The phase inset, the dashed tail in panel~(d) and the width curve in
panel~(e) are schematic. The plotting script (SHA-256 prefix
\texttt{ff60bc0f6b6083d3}) reads the plotted coordinates directly from
the archived fields. Before rendering it compares every plotted location
with the value displayed in Table~\ref{tab:ladder} to one unit of the
last displayed digit, derives each evidence class from that table's
evidence column, and compares the certified midpoints with
Table~\ref{tab:proof-cases}. These are consistency checks of the
rendering, not additional evidence for the physics. Its receipt records
the full checksums of script, inputs and outputs. Script, receipt and
input copies are published in the folder \texttt{fig-overview} of the
preprint 0.52 sources; they are not part of the 0.51 archive. In the
published copies, three lines of the script's header text and the host field
of the receipt are replaced by neutral wording; a map there lists the
checksums of the run copies and of the published copies.\par}
